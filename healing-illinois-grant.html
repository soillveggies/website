Hydroponics · Healing IL Grant

Healing IL Grant

Hydroponic towers funded by the Healing Illinois Grant bring year-round, soil-free growing into community and classroom spaces.

Hydroponic tower unit

Tower Overview

Vertical hydroponic towers grow leafy greens and herbs year-round using a fraction of the space and water of traditional beds.

Students learning at a tower

Educational Impact

The towers double as a teaching tool, giving students and community members a hands-on look at how hydroponic systems work.

Healing IL Grant recognition

Community Impact

Supported by the Healing Illinois Grant, this project extends growing seasons and access to fresh greens in the community.
