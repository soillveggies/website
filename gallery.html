Connecting the live Google Sheet feed

This gallery reads from a Google Sheet published as JSON through a small Apps Script (see gallery-apps-script.gs in the project root). Add rows to the Sheet — title, category, alt, and an optional imageUrl — and they'll appear here automatically, no redeploy needed. If the feed URL in js/main.js is left blank or is unreachable, this page falls back to the placeholder entries in data/gallery.json.
