Indoor Grow Operation

A rack of grow lights, a rotation of microgreen trays, and a pipeline that ends on a salad bar. This is where So ILL Veggies grows food indoors, year-round, regardless of weather.

Microgreens → Salad Bar

Trays move from seed to harvest in as little as one to two weeks, feeding a steady supply into a salad-bar pipeline for the community.
